# Enrollments

During registration, families pick a participation level for each eligible ministry.

## Enrollment types

- **Enrolled** — the child is expected at the program; they appear on the roster and at check-in.
- **Interest only** — the family is exploring the program. Leaders can follow up without treating the child as a regular attendee.

Which options appear depends on how the ministry is configured. See [Program setup](/help/user-guide/ministry-management/configuration).

## Where to review

- **Rosters** — who is enrolled in a ministry
- **Household** (guardian) — enrollments grouped by registration year
- **Ministries** (admin) — program settings that control what families can choose
