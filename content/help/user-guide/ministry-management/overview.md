# Ministry management overview

Administrators configure programs so families only see ministries that fit each child’s age and the current registration cycle.

![Ministry list](/help/screenshots/ministry-list.png)

## What you can configure

- Ministry name, description, and whether it is active
- Age or grade eligibility
- Enrollment type: enrolled, interest-only, or both
- Custom questions asked during registration
- Leader assignments

Details: [Program setup](/help/user-guide/ministry-management/configuration) and [Enrollments](/help/user-guide/ministry-management/enrollments).

## Leader access

Ministry leaders see rosters and check-in for ministries they are assigned to. Administrators can assign leaders from **Ministries** and **Leaders**.

## Safety

Only grant leader access to people who should see child and household information for that ministry.
