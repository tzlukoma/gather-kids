# Competitions

A Bible Bee **year** (or cycle) holds divisions, scripture lists, and enrollments.

Leaders typically:

1. Open **Bible Bee**
2. Select the year
3. Review divisions and assigned scriptures
4. Confirm the right children are enrolled

Guardians do not edit competition setup. They follow the assignments leaders publish for that year.
