# Child profiles

Every child in the household has a profile used for ministry placement, check-in, and safety.

## Basic information

- First and last name
- Date of birth (used for age-appropriate ministries)
- Grade
- Optional photo to help leaders identify children at check-in

![Child profile fields](/help/screenshots/registration-child-profile.png)

## Medical and safety notes

Please be specific:

- Allergies (food and environmental)
- Special needs or accommodations
- Anything pickup volunteers should know

This information can appear on check-in cards. Keep it accurate before Sunday.

## Ministry enrollments

Enrollments are stored **by registration year**. On the household page, open the year accordion under the child to see enrolled programs versus interest-only. See [Manage your household](/help/user-guide/registration/household-management).

## Photos

Photos should be recent, show the child’s face clearly, and be appropriate for ministry use. Leaders with permission can also update a photo from check-in.
