# Check children in

1. Sign in as a ministry leader or administrator.
2. Open **Check-In**.
3. Choose the event if prompted.
4. Find the child (search by name if the list is long).
5. Review allergy badges and notes on the card.
6. Select **Check In**.

![Check-in list](/help/screenshots/check-in-list.png)

![Successful check-in](/help/screenshots/check-in-success.png)

## Filters

Use status filters (**All**, **Checked In**, **Checked Out**) and grade filters when the room is large.

## If a family is missing

They may not be enrolled in this ministry or this registration cycle. Ask an administrator to confirm the household registration rather than checking in from memory.
