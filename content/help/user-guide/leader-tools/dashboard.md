# Dashboard

Administrators land on **Overview** (also called the dashboard). It summarizes:

- Households and children registered for the active cycle
- How many children are currently checked in
- Unacknowledged incidents that need attention

![Leader dashboard](/help/screenshots/leader-dashboard.png)

Use the sidebar to jump to Check-In, Rosters, Incidents, Ministries, and Bible Bee.

Ministry leaders who are not administrators may go straight to Check-In or Rosters depending on their role.
