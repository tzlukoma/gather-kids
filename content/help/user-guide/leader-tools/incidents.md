# Incidents

Use **Incidents** when something happens that families or leadership should know about — injury, behavior, or a safety concern.

![Incidents list](/help/screenshots/leader-incidents.png)

## Good practice

- Write facts, not blame
- Note who was present and what you did next
- Follow up until the incident is acknowledged

Incident details include child information. Treat them as confidential. The dashboard also flags unacknowledged incidents for administrators.
