# Reports

**Reports** summarizes attendance and participation so leaders do not have to reconstruct Sundays from memory.

Use reports together with [rosters](/help/user-guide/leader-tools/rosters) and [check-in](/help/user-guide/check-in-out/overview) when you need counts for a date range or ministry.

Export options depend on your role. If a report looks empty, confirm the registration cycle and that check-in was used for those gatherings.
